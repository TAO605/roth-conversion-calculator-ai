# V1.3 Engineering Addendum - Professional Handoff Packet

Date: 2026-05-30

This addendum implements the next V1.3 trust and conversion step: helping users move from an educational calculator scenario to a qualified professional review.

## Implemented

- Added `buildProfessionalHandoffText` to generate a deterministic plain-text review packet.
- Added `CopyProfessionalHandoffButton` and mounted it in the homepage result action row as `Copy CPA packet`.
- Included calculator inputs, modeled outputs, input-triggered tax-impact review items, source documents to bring, and the required disclaimer.
- Reused `buildTaxImpactReviewItems` so the packet matches the result-adjacent warning panel.
- Added tests for packet content, clipboard behavior, homepage mounting, and high-risk language boundaries.

## Compliance Boundary

- The packet is not tax, financial, legal, or investment advice.
- The packet does not say a user should convert, does not label an amount as optimal, and does not claim guaranteed accuracy.
- The packet supports a discussion with a qualified professional and avoids collecting advisor contact details or personal documents.

## UX Rationale

- Plain text is easy to paste into email, notes, tax organizer portals, or advisor intake forms.
- Copying locally preserves the site's privacy posture: no account, no upload, no server-side storage of financial inputs.
- Keeping the action near `Share result` and `Download report` makes the handoff available at the exact moment users review the estimate.
