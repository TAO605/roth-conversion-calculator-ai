# V1.3 Advanced Preview Content Boundaries Addendum

Date: 2026-06-10
Version: 1.0.214

## Purpose

The calculator now includes several bounded review previews that did not exist in the original MVP copy. User-facing guide pages and tax-interaction pages must describe those previews accurately without implying final tax advice or professional tax-form completion.

## Updated Content Boundary

ACA premium tax credit:

- The calculator may show an APTC at-stake preview when the user enters annual advance premium tax credit and Marketplace coverage months.
- The calculator does not determine Marketplace eligibility, final premium tax credit, repayment caps, benchmark plan premiums, poverty-line percentage, or Form 8962 outcomes.

NIIT:

- The calculator may show a bounded 3.8% NIIT preview when the user enters net investment income.
- The preview uses the lesser of user-entered net investment income or the calculator MAGI proxy excess.
- The calculator does not classify investment income, deductions, trade or business exceptions, credits, or final Form 8960 results.

RMD:

- The calculator may show a bounded Uniform Lifetime Table preview when entered age and traditional IRA balance fit the retained preview range.
- The calculator does not determine final RMD obligations, prior December 31 adjusted balances, account type, inherited-account schedules, spouse-beneficiary table treatment, custodian sequencing, or conversion order.

## Implementation Rule

When a bounded preview is implemented, update these surfaces in the same round:

- The guide content source under `src/content`.
- The guide page header copy under `src/app`.
- The tax interaction hub data.
- FAQ and methodology scope copy when the change affects global boundaries.
- Release notes, task tracking, and regression tests.

## Guardrail

Avoid stale blanket phrases such as "not modeled" or "does not estimate" for modules that now have a bounded preview. Prefer specific language:

- "bounded preview"
- "from user-entered inputs"
- "not a final tax-form result"
- "outside the core conversion tax calculation"
- "requires separate professional, IRS, Marketplace, SSA, custodian, or tax software review"
- "preview stays pending until the required user inputs are provided"
- "needs Marketplace, benefit, net investment income, or Form 6251 inputs"

Do not add advanced preview values to the main `totalUpfrontCost` or imply final tax owed, final credit eligibility, final RMD obligation, or personal tax advice.

## Missing-Input Copy Rule

When a bounded preview exists but the user has not entered the required optional fields, describe the module as pending input rather than unavailable. Acceptable examples:

- "The bounded APTC at-stake preview stays pending until Marketplace-specific inputs are provided."
- "The bounded 3.8% NIIT preview stays pending until net investment income is provided."
- "The bounded taxable-benefit preview stays pending until SSA-1099 benefit and Publication 915 inputs are provided."
- "The AMT exposure preview stays pending until Form 6251 comparison inputs are provided."

This keeps the product state accurate: the preview feature exists, but final tax-form or agency determinations remain outside the calculator.
